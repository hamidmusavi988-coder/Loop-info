How to swap TON for LOOP.

LOOP trades on STON.fi, a decentralized exchange on TON. You swap straight from your own wallet.

  1. Get a TON wallet

    Use a wallet such as Tonkeeper. Write your recovery phrase on paper and keep it private. No one from this project will ever ask for it.

  2. Add some TON

    You swap TON for LOOP, and each swap also needs a little extra TON for the network fee.

  3. Open the swap on STON.fi

    The button opens the LOOP pool on STON.fi. Connect your wallet there, then tap Trade.

    Swap TON for LOOP

  4. Check the address, then swap

    Make sure the LOOP contract address matches the one on the Token tab. Start with a small amount, look at the price impact and the minimum received, then confirm in your wallet.

    Token details

Before you swap

  • The pool is tiny, so a small trade can move the price a lot.
  • The contract has not had an independent security audit.
  • LOOP is not verified by Tonkeeper, and some wallets show a warning label on it.
  • You can lose everything you put in. Only use money you can afford to lose.
